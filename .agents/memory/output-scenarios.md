---
name: Output scenario mapping
description: Interpretation of legacy supplied forecast blocks when exporting the three fixed scenarios.
---
The supplied “Improvement” forecast is the primary plan case, not an independently calculated Conservative or Ambitious forecast.

**Why:** The imported examples predate the three fixed scenario definitions. Relabeling their one forecast for every selection would imply calculations that have never been performed.

**How to apply:** Explain the mapping in exports. An alternate scenario needs its own explicitly matched forecast and targets; otherwise show Data needed while retaining scenario-independent baseline facts and actions.
