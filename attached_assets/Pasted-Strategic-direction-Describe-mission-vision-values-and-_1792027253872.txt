Vision
We want to become a world-class, sustainable waterworks by 2035. We want to achieve highly potable, 24/7 water supply. We see a more effective waterworks company capable of providing reliable and safe/clean water to every household in Tagbilaran City.
Mission
Our mission is to provide our customers in 10/15 barangays in Tagbilaran City with safe and reliable water. Our employees are capable and highly trained. We strive to treat every customer fairly and respectfully and address their needs quickly and effectively.

Increase billed volume by expanding to unserved barangays, upgrading pumping stations and replacing inaccurate meters.

Billed volume (m3/yr) and connections; baseline 1,833,134 m3 (2024), 6,314 connections (2025).

OBJ-2
Owner
Smart
Relevant
NRW above the draft JMC standard (20% or below).
Specific
Reduce physical and commercial losses through DMAs, pipe replacement and detection of illegal connections.
Achievable
Measurable
NRW (%) = (production - billed)/production; baseline 31.3% (H1 2025), target 20%.
Time bound
Proposed path from demand analysis: 26% by 2030, 20% by 2035 (to be confirmed).
Title
Reduce NRW to 20%
