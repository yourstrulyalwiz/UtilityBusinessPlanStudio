========================================
DESIGN SYSTEM — MANDATORY FOR BOTH OUTPUTS
========================================
Less is more
- One idea per page or slide. If a page needs two ideas, split it or move one to the annex.
- Titles are messages, not labels: a short sentence with the finding ("Losses fall by half by 2030"), not "NRW analysis".
- Numbers: show only the 3 that matter most per page or slide; round them (one decimal for %, millions with "M"); always show units; detailed tables go to the annexes.
- Text limits: document bullets max 15 words; slide body max 25 words in total; no paragraphs on slides.
- Prefer a visual over a table and a table over text.

Layout
- Generous white space: margins of at least 2.5 cm in the document and 0.5 in on slides.
- 12-column grid; consistent alignment; left-aligned text; no centered body text.
- The same template for all modules, so the reader always knows where to look: header, key message, figures, visual, bullets, sources.
- Every module is clearly identified: number badge, module color and phase tag in the same position on every page or slide.

Typography
- Use the brand fonts only. Clear hierarchy, max 3 sizes per page: title, subtitle/key message, body.
- Headline figures large and bold (slides 40–60 pt); labels small and in the secondary text color.
- No underlining, no all-caps paragraphs, no more than one bold phrase per bullet.

Color
- Brand palette only. Primary color for the cover, headers and emphasis; module colors only as accents (badge, rule, chart highlight).
- At most 3 colors per chart; highlight the one series that carries the message and gray the rest.
- Status colors reserved for meaning: green (on track), amber (at risk or data needed), red (off track). Never use color as the only signal; add a label or icon.
- Minimum contrast WCAG AA.

Charts
- One message per chart, stated in its title.
- No 3D, no shadows, no gradients, no pie charts with more than 4 slices.
- Light or no gridlines; direct labels on bars and line ends instead of legends when possible; units in the axis title.
- Same chart style across document and deck.

Icons and images
- One consistent line-icon set in brand color; no clip art or stock photos unless the utility uploaded its own.
- Utility photos (if uploaded) only on the cover, section dividers or the closing slide.

Consistency and finish
- Footer on every page or slide: utility name · plan period · page or slide number; sources in small type.
- The same names for modules, phases and indicators as in the app.
- Language: plain, active, no jargon; spell out acronyms the first time.
